## A note on epistemic status

These records span a wide range of confidence. Some contain results that have been
independently verified and survive adversarial testing; others are exploratory and
state conjectures that have not been established.

The most recent record (Darmiyan v4 v2) is a **corrective revision** that retracts
its own central empirical attribution. Readers evaluating this body of work should
treat that document's retraction section as the intended standard: claims are stated
with their controls, and withdrawn when the controls do not support them.

Where a repository accompanies a paper, the code is the authority — not the prose.
